IntuneApp — Deploy Windows Apps via Intune

Create, package, and publish Windows applications to your Intune endpoints with a simple menu-driven tool. IntuneApp handles detection, install, uninstall, and requirements logic so you don't have to.

  • Pre-packaged installers for 30+ common apps (Chrome, 7-Zip, Notepad++, and more)
  • Bulk install apps across your fleet in minutes
  • Org Prep wizard connects to your Entra tenant automatically
  • Assign apps via Entra Groups — no complex Intune policy needed
